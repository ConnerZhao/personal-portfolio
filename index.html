Aloud

Human-sounding text-to-speech in the menu bar. Paste text, press Speak. It runs fully offline on the Kokoro-82M model, so there's no API key and nothing leaves the laptop.

  • 0.6 sfrom Speak to first audio, at any length
  • ~4×faster than real time on Apple Silicon

02Projects

Machine Learning · Personal Project

Predictive Sleep Analytics Model

XGBoost · SHAP · Streamlit

Predicts sleep duration, quality, and deep-sleep share from daily habits, shipped as a Streamlit dashboard where you move sliders and SHAP shows what is helping or hurting. I found and fixed a data-leakage bug that had pushed classifier accuracy to a fake 100%.

  • R² 0.69on sleep quality score
  • 100,000rows × 32 columns modeled
